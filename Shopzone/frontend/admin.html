ShopZone Shield

Three layers between a card and a criminal

LAYER 01
🔢

Structure + Luhn validation

Every card number is checked for a valid 16-digit layout and passes the Luhn checksum before it ever reaches the model. Bad cards are declined and struck — three strikes suspend the account.

LAYER 02
📏

Rule engine

Repeated failed attempts (+40), late-night hours (+20), unknown card networks (+30) and high-value orders (+25/+40) accumulate risk points — an explainable baseline that never sleeps.

LAYER 03
🧠

RandomForest ML model

A scikit-learn RandomForest trained on the transaction dataset scores amount, country, hour and failed attempts. Score ≥ 80 blocks the payment, 50–79 flags it suspicious, and every verdict is logged.
